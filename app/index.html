    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone, in the order the game asks for things: story first, then the stranger strands, then activities and collectibles, then crime and money, then the 100% cleanup. Each phase lists the trophies it covers and how many of them you have earned.

      Missable trophies

      No trophy in the official list is confirmed missable by its description alone — the game only released on 19 November 2026 and mission replay has not been confirmed in-game. The trophies below are the ones whose descriptions set a condition that can fail on an attempt (an undetected robbery, a back-to-back chain, a single use of a skill), so play them carefully and, where the game lets you, keep a save before you try. Story and chapter trophies are listed separately as unmissable on a replayable story, with the same warning until replay is confirmed.
